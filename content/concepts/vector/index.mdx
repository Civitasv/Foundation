# Vector

## Why it exists

Transformers do not operate on words directly. They operate on numerical representations, and vectors are the first useful abstraction for understanding those representations.

## Interactive target

Move a vector in two dimensions, inspect its coordinates, magnitude, direction, and dot products.

## Connection to agents

Agent behavior is produced by models whose internal state is represented numerically. Vectors are the first layer beneath embeddings, attention, retrieval, and similarity.
